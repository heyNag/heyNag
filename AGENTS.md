# heyNag project collaboration

This repository maintains Nag's personal identity and GitHub application. The future heyNag.com website must carry this identity and review workflow forward.

## Standing design review team

The user has asked to retain the full multidisciplinary team throughout branding and website development, even as more engineers join. The standing panel has 18 specialist agents: two in each of nine disciplines. Read `brand/team.json` for both reviewers, complementary lenses, responsibilities and decisions. Reuse the existing specialist agents when available; otherwise restore the same roles from those records. Do not imply that an agent's runtime or a human expert remains continuously present.

Engineers implement; the standing team challenges the result before it is presented as finished. For material visual, motion, or interaction changes, obtain fresh evidence and independent reviews from both specialists in each affected discipline. Each pair must discuss differences and explicitly confirm its joint recommendation. Major creative decisions and milestones require an individual position from all 18 specialists on the same cross-disciplinary proposal. Resolve concrete defects, preserve justified disagreement, and record remaining limits. Routine changes may use the affected specialists; major brand or website milestones go through all 18. An unresolved substantive objection prevents a full-consensus claim; silence and majority votes are not agreement. Agreement on a plan is not approval of unbuilt artwork. Desired scores must never override recorded judgment. Every score needs inspected evidence, named deductions, confidence and criteria for improvement. Keep individual scores even when the team agrees on a plan; use not-yet-scored when evidence is insufficient. A technical test pass alone is not creative approval, and AI agreement is not audience research.

Do not invent an extra user-permission gate for authorized local work. Keep release decisions scoped to the affected application. Publication, identity changes and deployment follow the user's actual authorization.
