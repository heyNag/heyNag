Contact can carry feeling.

The implementation brief.

Start with the known reading-key issue and the signature acting study. Develop tightly scoped visual and timing alternatives alongside the unchanged control.

P1 · Make one behavior feel recognizably Nag
  1. Place the current curiosity sequence beside one fixed, meaningful cue from a verified project question. Keep the content understandable without motion. Record what attention, investigation and sharing are intended to communicate.
  2. Compare the unchanged baseline with a purposeful investigation/share variant and, where useful, a restrained wry variation. Use comparable narrative scope, not forced identical milliseconds. Do not add a new world, seasonal scene or larger anatomy.
  3. Make geekiness visible through careful attention; glasses support it. Make talkative warmth visible through address, a pause or an afterthought; sound and constant mouth movement are unnecessary.

What earns adoption

  • Attention has a readable subject and the return toward the visitor conveys sharing at the intended size.
  • The gesture and content do not imply an unverified success or failure.
  • Nag can respond to concrete variants for personal fit; that preference remains separate from stranger recognition.
  • Both specialists identify why the chosen version is more distinctive or clear; name contradictory observations and preserve the baseline.
P2 · Give the priority actions distinct timing and staging
  1. Inspect current Curious, Thinking, Mischievous, Surprised and Excited silently, unlabelled and at normal speed. Separate first readable response, pause, reveal and settlement from total runtime.
  2. Audition a one-peek mischievous alternative against the existing two-peek behavior; specify whether the second peek adds renewed checking or dilutes the joke. Assess its static fallback separately.
  3. Try narrowly scoped timing alternatives where useful: curious entry from rest versus replay, surprise onset, and head recovery around hop contact. Pre-contact compression is not inherently wrong; changes need a visible benefit.
  4. Refine intended hand/ruff contacts or negative space in Thinking, Focused, Grateful, Shy and Worried. Clasped gratitude may retain contact. No longer arms or enlarged hands.
  5. Review replay and selected interruptions, including an airborne change and a reflective-to-joy switch when relevant. Cancelled commands need not complete their emotional beat.

What earns adoption

  • A short first viewing shows the intended order and selected actions have distinguishable rhythms.
  • Hop descent, contact and recovery read coherently without detached feet, unwanted bounce or adult proportions.
  • No accidental cheerful reset in the inspected compatible transition.
  • Selected gestures remain readable in both themes, and the unchanged control remains available for comparison.
P3 · Resolve drawing, material and scale choices
  1. Compare matched poses at equal visible height: baseline, a quieter local night antenna edge, and reduced whole-character rim as separate experiments. Preserve the repaired stem connection.
  2. Compare a restrained cream/forewing tonal-falloff variant for softness. Additional texture, blur, fur marks, shine or glow are not requirements.
  3. Inspect actual-size 32/48/64px avatars, 96/128/160px full figures and the 390px phone header; use a larger matched reference to diagnose.16/24px are optional boundary probes, not newly mandated uses.
  4. Keep the documented paper-ground day avatar as the universal GitHub account option. Treat the night avatar as a separate owned-surface variant. Preserve the readable wordmark and lockup unless a specific alternative proves beneficial.

What earns adoption

  • Both antenna paddles and the face remain framed; intended stems connect and the facial/hand contours stay readable on supported grounds.
  • Any quieter edge or softer material improves a named quality without muddy gestures, glossy cream or distracting halos.
  • Record supported sizes, grounds, application choice and native-vector provenance.
  • Species identification is not a compulsory brand goal; owner association remains a separate research question.
P4 · Make the studio comfortable and reliably operable
  1. Reproduce and correct document-wide Space/arrow interception while reading outside the player. Remove those shortcuts or scope retained shortcuts to a deliberate, explained player context; visibility alone is insufficient.
  2. Actually open, navigate and commit a different native feeling option. Verify selected value, heading, status and focus; the previous probe was inconclusive, not evidence of breakage.
  3. Retest gallery focus/next Tab, previous/next wrapping, pause/resume/rest, collection cancellation and live reduced-motion changes. Add bounded screen-reader speech and zoom/reflow observations.
  4. Compare Both versus single-theme mobile viewing for discoverability and actual figure size. Keep control usability separate from emotion recognition; change defaults only for observed benefit.

What earns adoption

  • Reading keys outside deliberate playback retain ordinary browser behavior.
  • Native controls and visible focus remain intact; selection and status describe the same action.
  • Reduced motion provides meaningful stills and timeline inspection, with no unsolicited motion.
  • Document actual assistive-technology and mobile results without a blanket compliance or recognition claim.
P5 · Prove every adopted refinement in the renderer
  1. Reuse the existing 504-pose geometry comparison, numeric tests and exact interruption samples as baseline coverage. Add only the renderer, placement or playback checks needed by the adopted changes.
  2. Inspect selected contact/interruption states at actual use sizes in both themes. Check clipping, false appendages, facial obstruction, theme parity and finite animation cleanup.
  3. Keep editable variants, source revisions, export recipes and before/after evidence together; regenerate affected application assets and the checksum manifest.

What earns adoption

  • Observed defects have narrow regression checks plus rendered proof.
  • Geometry/pose parity, tiny anatomy, reduced-motion behavior and finite endings survive the adopted changes.
  • Passing tests is reported as reliability evidence, not artistic or audience approval.
P6 · Validate meaning and rescore honestly
  1. Each specialist reviews implemented evidence independently before reading the partner’s new score. Each pair discusses concrete differences and records both positions.
  2. Use unprimed formative observers when testing comprehension or association: free descriptions before trait menus or labels; meaningful synonyms are acceptable. Test only distinctions that matter in the intended application.
  3. For delayed recall, either report it as occurring after an immediate probe or reserve a separate group for its first memory probe later. Keep recognition alternatives and character clips after that person’s final unaided recall probe.
  4. Record conditions, familiarity, actual responses, denominators, dissent and missing follow-ups. No recruited people or findings currently exist. Research can run alongside authorized local refinement; it is not a universal gate on unrelated work.
  5. Keep silence complete. Optional sound requires separate scope, explicit opt-in, easy mute, sparse non-overlapping cues and repeat-listening review; no audio is needed to earn quality in the present silent foundation.

What earns adoption

  • Every awarded score has evidence and an explicit account of what improved or still falls short.
  • A review claiming 9 or above addresses its named deductions with implemented, freshly inspected work. A lower or withheld score stays visible.
  • All 18 register a position on the same implemented scope; substantive objections are resolved or remain explicitly open.
  • Human response, owner preference and expert craft judgments remain distinct.
